**Owner LAB observation:** November 18, 2026, immediately after installing exact **1.5.0 development preview at commit 7fa7951f4a45f6e2a344856f3a715134d50f0fd8** into the copied LAB. The player screenshot shows multiple large and small magenta/black 2×2 missing-texture patterns near an existing storage/Sorter array. **This is a real player-visible rendering defect.** The screenshot alone does *not* prove that the affected cube is the newly added Transporter, Bulk, legacy Sorter, filter display entity, a held filter item, or another pack's resource. Do not invent precise block IDs or demand specific XYZ.

## Source-backed findings (do not overstate)

- Investigated exact installed commit BP blocks, RP terrain atlas, RP models, RP manifests and archived evidence. The **Hart Transporter and Bulk block texture aliases are defined** in `addon/resource_pack/textures/terrain_texture.json`, and their corresponding **PNG files exist with valid PNG chunk CRC and decode successfully**. Their BP `minecraft:material_instances` names and geometry identifiers agree with RP content. Existing Sorter materials in BP also have matching texture atlas entries.
- The BP manifest requires RP header UUID `91316442-0f26-4fbb-88f3-2e39ea843a1a` at version **1.5.0**; installer output reported copying the RP and updating `ThomasServer/world_resource_packs.json`. That proves **server-side installation was attempted and reported complete**, *not* whether the **Minecraft client** fetched/accepted/activated it.
- New source regression [resource link and image integrity test](../runtime/tests/urce_150_resource_integrity.test.mjs) verifies **all** BP block material aliases, atlas-to-PNG files, CRC and DEFLATE decode, nonvanilla geometry identifiers, original filter-display client entity and BP/RP dependencies. This is offline **source integrity only**.
- Official BDS [server.properties](https://learn.microsoft.com/minecraft/creator/documents/bedrockserver/server-properties) documents `texturepack-required` **default false**; clients may connect without the server's texture pack. The owner LAB's actual setting is **NOT YET KNOWN**. Bedrock client **cached resources or competing global packs** also can lead to wrong assets even when source and installer are correct. Native Bedrock content logs and pack-download observations are required to differentiate.
- Content log diagnostics: [Bedrock troubleshooting guide](https://wiki.bedrock.dev/guide/troubleshooting) covers wrong texture paths, JSON and client cache. Do **not** "fix" the screenshot by deleting stored Sorters, changing block identities, clearing world data, or replacing original 1.4.2 assets without an identified fault.

## Read-only diagnostic and action sequence

- New [one-file copied-LAB resource doctor](../distribution/Diagnose-HartLogistics-LABResources.ps1) **reads only**: the exact expected LAB folder, active world name, BP/RP UUIDs and versions, RP dependency, world pack registration, block material aliases and atlas PNG signatures, retained filter-display texture and original geometry, plus `server.properties` `texturepack-required` and `content-log-file-enabled`. It never writes, stops BDS, restarts BDS, reads production or changes a client cache. It cannot prove that a *client* downloaded assets.
- If the doctor fails: retain the exact error message to find whether **installed** RP is incomplete or mismatched. Repair only with an appropriate versioned, protected updater after identifying the mismatch; **do not overwrite the same 1.5.0 registered pack with different bytes or bypass REFUSED safeguards**.
- If the doctor passes but `texturepack-required` is **false/missing**: client-side RP delivery is a primary hypothesis. A controlled LAB-only experiment may set `texturepack-required=true` after backing up `server.properties` and stopping BDS, then restart and reconnect to ensure clients accept the Hart RP; this is a **server setting change**, not performed here and not permission to alter production settings. The option may affect other packs, so inspect server/client pack stack first.
- If the doctor passes and the client confirms RP downloaded: inspect **client Settings → Creator → Content Log**, note the **actual missing texture/model identifier and pack name**, and check local cached server resource data and conflicting global packs. Do not indiscriminately delete all client resource data; avoid losing unrelated worlds or packs. A reconnection after confirming required pack may suffice.
- If a specific missing alias, entity texture or resource-cache defect is confirmed: fix that exact source issue and release a **new unique BP/RP version** (not reuse `[1,5,0]` under the same UUID), with an explicit verified **1.5.0 → next-version** PowerShell installer and rollback tests. The old 1.4.2 and 1.5.0 backup snapshots, including owner backup `lab-ad81804f1005`, remain protected.

**Status:** Screenshot = real BDS/client rendering failure. **Root cause OPEN** pending installed-LAB doctor output and client content log. CI asset-chain tests cannot certify a player's live client. The new tests and diagnostics add **no gameplay writers, pack format/version change, gameplay code change, or deployment action**.
