## Architecture correction from the evidence

1. **Native direct routing: demonstrated.** Whole-native-stack, exact empty-slot transfer with visible enchantment retention across a *normal restart* was accepted at PG20 scope. Do not classify native MR movement as untested, impossible or speculative. Do not generalize it to partial-stack splits, occupied-slot merges, opaque identity or power-cut consistency.
2. **Native persistent physical storage: demonstrated at bounded scope.** PG42 successfully exercised a **nine-slot native inventory entity** with named/lore ItemStack and entity-ID/slot persistence observed across a normal restart on the owner's no-experiments stable runtime. That proves a feasible native storage primitive, **not a complete protected Collector/Receiver custodian**.
3. **Custom-block storage availability: distinct unresolved/blocked path.** PG-03 NO-GO applies to one custom-block inventory candidate on 1.26.63.1; it does **not** negate the native entity inventory demonstrated by PG42.
4. **Full custody: not accepted.** Noncooperative vanilla/player/add-on writers, unload/death/identity management, in-flight journal vs physical inventory save order, exact opaque metadata witnesses, ambiguous crash HOLD, old-world Q4 preservation and full route integration still need evidence. Distinguish *absence of a proven guarantee* from *observed loss/duplication*.
5. **Present 1.4.1 risk: do not hide it.** The active 1.4.1 candidate uses the original partial-serialization/rebuild-style recovery boundary until separately amended and verified. PG19 (source) and PG20 (historical remediation) inform later MR hardening, **but are not imported implementations**. Baseline [Level 2 physical/journal decisions](NATIVE_TRANSFER_AND_CUSTODY_LEVEL2_DECISIONS.md) still apply.
